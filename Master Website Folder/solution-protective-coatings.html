The Problem: Chloride Corrosion Destroys Structures

Salt Air Eats Steel. Humidity Eats Paint. Coastal Structures Pay the Price.

Chloride ions from sea air penetrate concrete and reach reinforcing steel. Steel corrodes, expands, and spalls the concrete from inside. A structure that should last 50 years fails in 15. This is the single biggest maintenance cost for India's coastal infrastructure.

What Happens Without Protection

  • Chloride penetration — Salt air deposits chlorides on concrete. They migrate inward at 1–3 mm per year. Once they reach rebar, corrosion is inevitable.
  • Paint failure — Standard paints fail within 2–3 years in coastal environments. UV degrades the binder, humidity lifts adhesion, salt spray accelerates both.
  • Maintenance access cost — Bridge decks, offshore platforms, port infrastructure are expensive and dangerous to access. A coating that fails in 5 years costs 5× more over a 25-year life than one applied once correctly.

India has over 7,500 km of coastline and hundreds of billions in coastal infrastructure. Corrosion costs Indian industry an estimated ₹4 lakh crore annually.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the system work on both steel and concrete?

Yes. SBR-50 primer is formulated for both steel and concrete substrates. Preparation differs — steel requires blast cleaning, concrete requires laitance removal and open surface. The marine epoxy topcoats are identical for both.

What's the minimum temperature for application?

Minimum 10°C substrate and ambient temperature. Maximum 40°C. In South India's peak summer, apply early morning (6–10 AM) to avoid overheating during pot life window.

Can I apply without blast cleaning?

Yes — hand tool cleaning (wire brush, grinder) to St 3 is acceptable. However, blast cleaning to Sa 2.5 gives 40–60% longer system life. For critical infrastructure, blast cleaning is strongly recommended.

Is this system suitable for submerged structures?

For permanently submerged structures (jetty pilings, dock walls), use a minimum 3 coats of HE500 at 600–700 microns DFT total. Contact Hardex technical team for submerged application specifications.

How do I know when the system needs maintenance?

Inspect annually. Look for: chalking (UV breakdown of topcoat), blistering (moisture under coating), rust spots (primer failure). Surface maintenance coat of HE400 at year 8–10 extends system life significantly without full recoat.
