THE PROBLEM

Chloride Corrosion Destroys Structures.

Salt Air Eats Steel. Humidity Eats Paint. Coastal Structures Pay the Price.

Chloride ions from sea air penetrate concrete and reach reinforcing steel. Steel corrodes, expands, and spalls the concrete from inside. A structure that should last 50 years fails in 15.

What Happens Without Protection

  • Chloride penetration — Salt air deposits chlorides on concrete. They migrate inward at 1–3 mm per year. Once they reach rebar, corrosion is inevitable.
  • Paint failure — Standard paints fail within 2–3 years in coastal environments. UV degrades the binder, humidity lifts adhesion, salt spray accelerates both.
  • Maintenance access cost — Bridge decks, offshore platforms, port infrastructure are expensive and dangerous to access. A coating that fails in 5 years costs 5× more over a 25-year life.

India has over 7,500 km of coastline. Corrosion costs Indian industry an estimated ₹4 lakh crore annually.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

How far from the coast does a structure need marine-grade coating?
Structures within 5 km of the coast are at risk from salt-laden air. Within 1 km, use full marine epoxy system. 1–5 km, minimum SBR-50 primer plus single coat HE500. Beyond 5 km, industrial-grade epoxy is adequate.
Does the system work on both steel and concrete?
Yes. SBR-50 primer is formulated for both steel and concrete substrates. Preparation differs — steel requires blast cleaning, concrete requires laitance removal. The marine epoxy topcoats are identical for both.
What is DFT and why does it matter?
DFT = Dry Film Thickness, measured in microns. Marine environments require 400–500 microns DFT minimum. Thinner coatings allow chloride ions to diffuse through to steel. Each coat of HE500 delivers 200–250 microns DFT.
Can I apply without blast cleaning?
Yes — hand tool cleaning (wire brush, grinder) to St 3 is acceptable. However, blast cleaning to Sa 2.5 gives 40–60% longer system life. For critical infrastructure, blast cleaning is strongly recommended.
How do I know when the system needs maintenance?
Inspect annually. Look for: chalking (UV breakdown of topcoat), blistering (moisture under coating), rust spots (primer failure). Surface maintenance coat of HE400 at year 8–10 extends system life significantly without full recoat.
