The Problem: Why Pavers and Decorative Concrete Deteriorate

₹2 Lakh in Pavers Looks Like This After 2 Monsoons

Algae growth, staining, fading, efflorescence (white powder), weed growth in joints. What was a beautiful driveway or courtyard becomes maintenance-heavy and ugly within 2–3 years — without sealing.

What Unsealed Pavers Face in India

  • Monsoon algae growth — Porous pavers absorb moisture and grow algae within weeks of rain season. Green, black, and brown staining.
  • Efflorescence — Salts in cement migrate to the surface and form white deposits. Cannot be cleaned without acid — which damages the paver.
  • Colour fading — UV in Indian summers degrades pigments within 1–2 years. Coloured pavers turn grey.
  • Staining — Oil, tyre marks, rust, biological stains penetrate unsealed surfaces permanently.
  • Joint erosion — Rainwater washes jointing sand. Weeds establish. Surface becomes unstable.

Sealing is the 2-hour job that makes the 2-year installation last 10 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

My pavers have algae. Can I seal over it?

No. Seal over algae and you lock it in — it continues growing under the sealer and eventually lifts it. Treat with a diluted bleach or dedicated algae cleaner, allow 24 hours, rinse thoroughly, dry completely, then seal.

My pavers have turned white (efflorescence). Will sealer fix it?

Remove efflorescence with dilute acid wash (1:10 hydrochloric acid solution) first. Neutralise with water, allow 48 hours to dry. Then seal. Do not seal over efflorescence — the salts will continue to migrate and lift the sealer.

Can I apply PAVEGUARD myself without a contractor?

Yes. PAVEGUARD WB is particularly DIY-friendly — apply by roller like paint. PAVEGUARD SB requires good ventilation and solvent-resistant gloves. Both are straightforward for homeowners on driveways and small courtyards.

Will sealing make my surface slippery?

PAVEGUARD WB maintains natural texture — no slip risk. PAVEGUARD SB high-gloss can be slightly slippery when wet. For pool surrounds, wet areas, or ramps, add anti-slip aggregate to the final coat. Mention this when ordering.

My coloured pavers have faded. Can PAVEGUARD COLOR bring them back?

Yes — this is exactly what PAVEGUARD COLOR is designed for. The colour-enhancing resin penetrates the paver and deepens the pigment significantly. Results are best on concrete pavers. Natural stone responds less dramatically but still improves.
