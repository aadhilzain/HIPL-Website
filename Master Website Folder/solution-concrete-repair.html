THE PROBLEM

Bad Repairs Make It Worse.

A Cement Slap Over a Rusty Rebar Fixes Nothing

Within 2 years: the patch falls off, the rust has spread further along the bar, and you have a bigger repair. The cost of doing it wrong is always greater than doing it right the first time.

What Causes Structural Concrete Deterioration

  • Rebar corrosion — Most common failure mode. Corrosion products expand to 3–4× the volume of original steel, fracturing the concrete cover.
  • Carbonation — CO2 in air reacts with concrete, reducing pH. Below pH 11.5, rebar's passive protection breaks down.
  • Chloride penetration — Salt from sea air or deicing agents destroys rebar passivation. Aggressive in coastal India.
  • Honeycombing & voids — Poor concrete placement leaves air pockets. Water infiltrates and structure deteriorates from inside out.

REPAIR WORKFLOW

9–12 Day Structural Repair.

Day 1

Assessment & Break Out

Sound entire surface — identify all delaminated zones. Break out all compromised concrete to solid substrate, going 25mm behind rebar.

⏱ Scope-dependent · Don't leave any loose concrete
Day 1–2

Rebar Treatment

Wire brush all exposed rebar to bright metal (St 3). Apply RR100 rust remover — leave 4 hours. Apply CP200 corrosion inhibitor coat. Allow 12 hours.

⏱ 2–3 hrs application · 12 hrs cure
Day 2

SBR-50 Bond Coat

Wet substrate. Apply SBR-50 by brush to concrete and treated rebar. Apply mortar while still tacky — do not allow to dry fully.

⏱ 1 hr · Apply mortar while tacky
Day 2–4

RM40 Repair Mortar

Pack mortar in layers max 30mm. Compact each layer thoroughly — no voids. Allow 24 hrs between layers. Build to original profile.

⏱ 24 hrs per 30mm layer · Cure 7 days total
Day 9+

✓ Protective Topcoat

Apply COOLCOAT or AC200 over entire repaired area plus 200mm beyond. Seals surface against future water and CO2 ingress.

Total: 9–12 days for structural repair
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

How do I know if a crack is structural or cosmetic?
Cosmetic cracks are surface-only, hairline (less than 0.2mm), and don't pass through the element. Structural cracks are wider, through-going, and often diagonal (shear). If a crack is in a beam, column, or slab and you're unsure — get a structural engineer to assess before repair.
Can I fill cracks with epoxy injection instead of repair mortar?
Yes — for structural cracks where load transfer is needed, epoxy injection (HARDPOXY INJ200) restores monolithic concrete. Use repair mortar (RM40) for volume replacements (spalling). Use epoxy injection for hairline to 10mm cracks where structural continuity is the goal.
My building is 30 years old. Is repair worth it or should I demolish?
Assessment first — not assumption. A structural engineer can determine residual life. In most cases, buildings with rebar corrosion damage can be repaired for 20+ more years at 30–50% the cost of demolition and reconstruction.
How do I stop concrete columns from rusting again after repair?
Three layers: (1) CP200 on rebar — passive protective coating on steel. (2) RM40 repair mortar — lower permeability than original concrete. (3) HARDSEAL AC200 anti-carbonation topcoat — blocks CO2. All three together stop re-corrosion for 15–20 years.
