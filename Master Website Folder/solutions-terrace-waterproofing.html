THE PROBLEM

Why Terraces Fail Every Monsoon.

Every Monsoon, Your Terrace Leaks

Water stains on the ceiling. Top floor smells damp. Repainting doesn't help. Within 2–3 years, you're spending ₹50,000+ on repairs.

Root Causes of Terrace Failure

  • Concrete cracks — Microscopic cracks expand with temperature changes. Water finds them.
  • No adhesion layer — Previous paint peels off because there's no bonding primer.
  • Poor drainage — Standing water seeps through cracks and construction joints.
  • UV degradation — Sun breaks down unprotected coatings from the inside out.
  • Thermal cycling — Daily heat cycles crack the surface. Monsoon rains penetrate.

Most contractors apply one coat of paint. It lasts 18–24 months. They return. You pay again.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Can I apply this during monsoon season?
No. Concrete must be <4% moisture. Apply before monsoon (March–May) or during dry season (October–December).
What if my terrace is actively leaking right now?
Stop the source first. Hardproof INJ PU injection grout can plug active leaks in hours. Apply the full system after drying.
How do I verify the contractor applied correct coverage?
Each litre of SBR-50 covers 5–6.5 m². Each litre of membrane covers 0.5–0.7 m². Verify by weighing application with a scale.
Does the system work on old concrete with existing cracks?
Yes. Primer and membrane accommodate micro-cracks up to 1 mm. Wider cracks need filler first.
What is the warranty?
10 years against material defect. If applied per specification and maintained, the system prevents seepage.
