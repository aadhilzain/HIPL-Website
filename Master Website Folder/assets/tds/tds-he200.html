Technical Data Sheet Epoxy Systems · Bonding Agents
Product Description

Two-component 100% solids epoxy bonding agent for bonding new concrete, repair mortars, and overlays to existing concrete or masonry substrates. Creates a chemical weld at the interface with pull-off bond strength exceeding the tensile strength of concrete. Applied wet-on-wet for maximum adhesion.

StorageStore in dry conditions between 10°C–35°C. Avoid freezing.
Typical Uses

Bonding new concrete to old concrete in repair works. Interface agent for repair mortars, micro concrete, and overlays. Bonding epoxy screeds and toppings. Structural repair bonding in bridges, industrial floors, and building structures.

Application Notes & Precautions

Apply to both surfaces and allow to become tacky before placing fresh material — wet-on-wet application gives best results. If primer cures fully before overcoating, abrade and reapply. Mix in small batches to control pot life at site temperatures.
