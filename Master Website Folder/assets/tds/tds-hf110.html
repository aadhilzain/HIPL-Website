PRIME GUARD EP SB
Technical Data Sheet Flooring Systems · Epoxy Primers
Product Description

Two-component solvent-based epoxy primer designed for dense, power-trowelled, or laitance-affected concrete substrates. Superior penetration into tight surfaces where solvent-free primers may have limited absorption. Suitable for surface consolidation prior to epoxy coating systems.

Physical & Chemical Properties
Base2-Component Epoxy (solvented)
Mix Ratio2 : 1 by volume (A : B)
ColourTransparent / Light amber
FinishGloss
Solids by Volume45 ± 3%
VOC Content350–450 g/L
Application Data
Coverage6–8 m²/L
Application MethodRoller or brush
Pot Life (30°C)45–60 minutes
Touch Dry3–5 hours
Recoat Window6–24 hours
Full Cure5–7 days
Packaging & Storage
Packaging5 L, 20 L kits
Shelf Life12 months (unmixed, sealed)
StorageKeep away from heat, sparks, and open flame. Flammable.
Typical Uses

Dense or power-trowelled concrete surfaces. Damp-tolerant applications where light surface moisture is present. Surface consolidation before epoxy systems. Substrates where maximum penetration is critical.

Application Notes & Precautions

Ensure adequate ventilation — solvent vapours are flammable during application and cure. Apply on prepared substrate. Better penetration than SF on tight substrates. Keep away from ignition sources.
