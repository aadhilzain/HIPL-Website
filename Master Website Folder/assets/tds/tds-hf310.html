Technical Data Sheet Flooring Systems · Epoxy Roller Coatings
Product Description

Two-component solvent-containing epoxy roller coat for light to medium industrial floor applications. Provides a durable, dust-proof, chemical-resistant surface with a high-gloss finish. Applied by short-pile roller in two coats over primed concrete. Economical solution for warehouses, commercial spaces, and light manufacturing floors.

Physical & Chemical Properties
Base2-Component Epoxy
Mix Ratio2 : 1 by volume (A : B)
ColourStandard shades / Custom RAL available
FinishHigh gloss

VOC Content< 80 g/L

Pot Life (30°C)30 minutes

Recoat Window12–24 hours
Full Cure5–7 days
Packaging & Storage
Packaging6 kg, 15 kg kits
Shelf Life12 months (unmixed, sealed)
StorageStore in dry conditions between 10°C–35°C.
Typical Uses

Light industrial warehouse floors. Commercial showrooms and retail spaces. Light manufacturing and assembly areas. Pharmaceutical and food facility floors. Any application requiring a clean, dust-proof, professional floor finish.

Application Notes & Precautions

Apply in thin, uniform coats using a short-pile roller (9–12 mm). Two coats minimum over primed substrate. Avoid puddles and thick build-up. Substrate must be dry and primed with EP100 or MTP100.
