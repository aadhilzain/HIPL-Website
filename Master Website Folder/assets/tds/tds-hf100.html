PRIME GUARD EP SF
Technical Data Sheet Flooring Systems · Epoxy Primers
Product Description

Two-component, 100% solids solvent-free epoxy primer for industrial floor systems. Penetrates and seals concrete, providing a high-adhesion base for self-leveling epoxy, screed, and roller coat systems. Low VOC makes it suitable for food, pharmaceutical, and occupied environments.

Physical & Chemical Properties
Base2-Component Epoxy (low viscosity)
Mix Ratio2 : 1 by volume (A : B)
ColourClear / Pale amber
FinishGloss
Solids by Volume100% (solvent-free)
VOC Content< 50 g/L
Application Data
Coverage5–7 m²/kg depending on substrate porosity
Application MethodRoller or brush
Pot Life (30°C)30–40 minutes
Touch Dry5–7 hours
Recoat Window8–24 hours
Full Cure5–7 days
Packaging & Storage
Packaging6 kg, 15 kg kits
Shelf Life12 months (unmixed, sealed)
StorageStore in dry conditions between 10°C–35°C. Avoid freezing.
Typical Uses

Priming concrete before epoxy flooring systems. Concrete sealing and dust-proofing. High-build epoxy coating systems. Pharmaceutical and food-grade facilities where low VOC is mandatory.

Application Notes & Precautions

Apply only on dry, prepared surfaces (moisture < 4%). Mechanically prepare substrate by shot-blasting or diamond grinding. Mix Part A and Part B thoroughly for minimum 3 minutes before application. Apply within pot life window.
