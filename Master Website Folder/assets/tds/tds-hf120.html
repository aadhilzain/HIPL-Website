EP100
Technical Data Sheet Flooring Systems · Epoxy Primers
Product Description

Two-component epoxy primer for industrial floor coating systems on dry concrete substrates. High solids formulation provides excellent adhesion and a strong interface for all HARDFLOOR epoxy systems including SL1000, ERC200, ERC300, and TC500.

Physical & Chemical Properties
Base2-Component Epoxy
Mix Ratio2 : 1 by volume (A : B)
ColourTransparent / Amber
FinishGloss
Solids by Volume90 ± 2%
VOC Content< 100 g/L
Application Data
Coverage5–6 m²/kg
Application MethodRoller or brush
Pot Life (30°C)30 minutes
Touch Dry6 hours
Recoat Window8–24 hours
Full Cure5 days
Packaging & Storage
Packaging6 kg, 15 kg kits
Shelf Life12 months (unmixed, sealed)
StorageStore in dry conditions between 10°C–35°C.
Typical Uses

Primer coat before all HARDFLOOR epoxy flooring systems. General floor priming on dry concrete. Warehouses, factories, commercial spaces, and industrial facilities.

Application Notes & Precautions

Mix thoroughly before application. Apply only on dry substrate — for damp substrates use MTP100 (HF130). Mechanically prepare surface prior to priming. Do not overcoat beyond recoat window without abrading.
