MTP100
Technical Data Sheet Flooring Systems · Moisture Tolerant Primers
Product Description

Two-component moisture-tolerant epoxy primer formulated for application on damp or green concrete substrates. Bonds to concrete with up to 6% moisture content (ASTM F2170), enabling epoxy flooring systems to proceed without extended drying delays. Essential for new construction, post-rain applications, or high-humidity environments.

StorageStore above 10°C. Avoid freezing.
Typical Uses

Damp or green concrete floors. New construction where full concrete drying is not achievable. High-humidity industrial and basement environments. First coat in HARDFLOOR system applications over MTP100 before SL1000, ERC200, ERC300.

Application Notes & Precautions

Suitable for substrates with up to 6% moisture content. Mechanically prepare substrate before application — shot-blast or diamond grind. Mix thoroughly for minimum 3 minutes. Apply within pot life. Do not thin.
