THE PROBLEM

Leaking Tanks Cost You Every Day.

Every Drop Lost Is Money Lost

A 5,000-litre overhead tank losing 10% per day wastes 500 litres. At ₹5/litre for tanker water in many cities, that's ₹75,000+ per year. Plus structural damage from constant moisture.

Why Tanks Fail

  • Construction joints — Junction between base slab and walls is the primary failure point in most tanks.
  • Honeycombing — Poor concrete placement leaves voids that channel water.
  • Shrinkage cracks — Concrete cracks as it cures. These are highways for water.
  • No waterproofing layer — Many tanks are plain concrete with no protection.
  • Algae and biological growth — Destroys uncoated concrete surfaces over time.

Bitumen paint is the most common fix. It lasts 2–3 years maximum and is not safe for potable water. CM100 lasts 15+ years and is potable water approved.

THE SOLUTION

CM100 Rigid System.

CM100 is a rigid cementitious coating — it bonds chemically to concrete and creates a waterproof shell that resists both positive (water pushing in) and negative (water pushing out) hydrostatic pressure.

APPLICATION TIMELINE

28 Days to Potable Water Safe.

Day 1

Drain, Clean & Repair

Empty tank. Clean algae, lime scale, loose material. Use WP100 to stop any active seepage. Fill structural cracks with FASTFIX RM40.

⏱ 4–8 hrs depending on size
Day 1–2

SBR-50 Slurry Coat

Mix SBR-50 with cement (1:1.5 ratio). Apply as slurry to damp surface. Apply CM100 first coat while slurry is tacky — do not let dry.

⏱ 1–2 hrs · Proceed while tacky
Day 2

CM100 — First Coat

Apply 0.8–1.0 kg/m² by stiff brush. Work thoroughly into all corners and construction joints — highest risk areas.

⏱ 3–5 hrs · Cure: 24 hrs · Keep damp
Day 3

CM100 — Second Coat

Apply second coat perpendicular to first. For swimming pools, apply a third coat — total 2.0–2.5 kg/m². Keep moist during cure.

⏱ 3–5 hrs · Moist cure 3 days
Day 4–7

Moist Curing

Spray lightly with water 2–3 times daily. Do not allow to dry out — premature drying causes shrinkage cracks in CM100 itself.

⏱ 10 mins/day · Critical step — do not skip
Day 8+

Water Fill Test

Fill tank to 25% capacity. Check for seepage at 24 hrs and 48 hrs. If clear, fill to full.

⏱ 24 hr test per fill level
Day 28+

✓ Potable Water Safe

28 days full cure — tank is now safe for drinking water use. The cementitious coating is inert after cure, no harmful leaching.

Total: 28 days to potable water ready
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is CM100 safe for drinking water tanks?
Yes. CM100 is potable water safe after 28 days full cure. Do not use the tank for drinking water before this. The cementitious coating is inert after cure — no harmful leaching.
My tank has active water running through a crack. What first?
Use FASTFIX WP100 hydraulic cement first. Mix to putty consistency, press into the crack and hold for 60 seconds. It sets even with water flowing through. Only then proceed with CM100.
Can I apply CM100 to a tank that currently has water in it?
No. Tank must be drained and surface must be damp (not wet-running). Drain, clean, repair all active leaks, then allow surface to reach damp condition before applying.
How do I waterproof a swimming pool differently from a water tank?
Same system — CM100 — but apply 3 coats instead of 2 for pools (total 2.0–2.5 kg/m²). Pools face constant water pressure and foot traffic from swimmers. Allow full 28 days before filling.
What is the difference between CM100 and CM200 for water tanks?
CM100 is rigid and specifically designed for water-retaining structures under hydrostatic pressure — tanks, pools, reservoirs. CM200 is flexible for movement-prone wet areas like bathrooms. For water tanks, always use CM100.
