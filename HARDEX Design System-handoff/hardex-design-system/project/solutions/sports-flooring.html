THE PROBLEM

Most Outdoor Courts Fail in 3 Years.

A Cracked, Faded Court Damages the Game — and Damages Players

Uneven bounce from cracks causes wrong muscle memory. Slippery surfaces from UV-degraded coatings cause injuries. Faded lines cause disputes. A court that isn't maintained costs more in replacement than a properly built system.

Why Courts Fail Early

  • No primer coat — Acrylic coatings applied directly to concrete peel within 12–18 months.
  • Wrong number of coats — Single-coat applications thin out under UV. Minimum 2 coats of basecoat + 2 coats of topcoat.
  • Concrete not cured before coating — Applying to green concrete (less than 28 days) causes immediate adhesion failure.
  • No texture layer — Without basecoat texture, ball response is inconsistent and surface becomes slippery when wet.
THE SOLUTION

SPORTEYFLOR 4-Layer System.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Standard vs cushion system — which should I choose?
Standard for recreational courts, school courts, and low-frequency use. Cushion for club facilities, academies, and anywhere players train daily. The cushion layer significantly reduces long-term joint injury risk.
Can I apply SPORTEYFLOR over an existing acrylic court?
Yes, if the existing surface is well-bonded. Clean thoroughly, sand loose edges, apply SPORTEYFLOR PRIMER and proceed. If existing surface is peeling or delaminating, remove completely before applying new system.
What colours are available?
Standard colours: red, green, blue, terracotta for tennis; blue and red for badminton; custom colours on request. Line marking paint is available in white and yellow.
How do I maintain a SPORTEYFLOR court?
Sweep regularly. Wash with mild detergent and water annually. Reapply topcoat only (no need to redo full system) every 4–5 years to maintain colour and UV protection.
